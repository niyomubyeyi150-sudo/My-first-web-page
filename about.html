Our Mission

At EcoPulse, our mission is to empower educational and institutional campuses with real-time, data-driven visibility into their power distribution, renewable generation, and overall carbon footprint. By replacing fragmented tracking with automated grid analytics, we provide facility operators and sustainability leaders with the actionable intelligence required to eliminate energy waste, balance peak loads, and accelerate the transition toward net-zero carbon emissions
